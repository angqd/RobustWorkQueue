Scripted scenarios

Each one resets the queue, then runs its steps through the API.

1. Happy path

Submit 2 jobs, reserve both, complete both.

2. Expired lease + late complete

Reserve (token 1), advance 31s, complete with token 1 (rejected), re-reserve (token 2), complete.

3. Retries to DLQ

Submit 1 job, reserve and fail it 3 times. It ends in DEAD.

4. Capacity

Submit 5, a 6th gets QueueFull. Reserve and complete one, then the submit succeeds.
